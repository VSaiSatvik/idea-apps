Vouch Crawls — Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. Vouch Crawls is made by Sai Satvik ("we"). This page explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, so you can sign in.
  • For venues: venue name, address, offer details and the venue's location, which you set by tapping 'Use my current location'.
  • For creators: your public social handles, follower details you enter, and your advertiser permit number for verification.
  • Crawl activity: which crawls you joined, when you went live, and offer redemptions recorded at the venue's till.

How it works

  • Location is used only when you tap the button to set a venue's location. We do not track your location in the background.
  • Venues on a crawl can see the creator's public handle; creators can see the venue's public details.

Why we use it

Only to run the features of Vouch Crawls: signing you in, showing your data across your devices, and keeping the service safe. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes it

Our server is hosted by Render (our server provider). Data is stored there and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We share data with no one else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Venue and creator profiles are kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and the data linked to it are deleted from our server.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. Or email saisatvik.apps@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

Vouch Crawls is not intended for anyone under 18.
