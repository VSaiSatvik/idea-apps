AskOnce Circles — Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 October 2026. AskOnce Circles is made by Sai Satvik ("we"). This page explains what the app collects and why.

What we collect

  • Your name, email address and a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, so you can sign in.
  • The circles you create or join, and the questions and answers you choose to share into a circle. Shared answers are visible to every member of that circle.
  • Reports and blocks you make, so we can hide content and keep circles safe.

How it works

  • Your AI provider API key (Anthropic, OpenAI or Google) is stored only in your phone's secure storage. It is never sent to our server.
  • When you ask a question with your own key, the question goes directly from your phone to the provider you picked, under that provider's terms. We do not see it unless you share the answer into a circle.
  • If you use the 'try without a key' demo, your question is sent to our server and, if enabled, to Anthropic to generate the answer. Demo questions are not stored after the answer is returned.

Why we use it

Only to run the features of AskOnce Circles: signing you in, showing your data across your devices, and keeping the service safe. We do not sell your data, show ads, or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Who processes it

Our server is hosted by Render (our server provider). Data is stored there and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We share data with no one else except where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Members can delete their own entries. Circle owners can remove entries and members. When you delete your account, your account and the data linked to it are deleted from our server.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account → Delete account. Or email saisatvik.apps@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete it within 30 days.

Children

AskOnce Circles is not intended for anyone under 18.
